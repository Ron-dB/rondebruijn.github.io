## About

I am an Assistant Professor at Eindhoven University of Technology working on **design principles for precision mechatronic systems**. My research focuses on connecting modeling, control, and learning to enable predictable performance in high‑tech motion systems.

My work is motivated by applications such as semiconductor manufacturing equipment, precision positioning systems, and advanced instrumentation.

## Research Interests

- Precision mechatronics
- Motion control and feedforward design
- Data‑driven modeling and identification
- Learning‑based control methods
- Design methodologies for high‑tech systems
